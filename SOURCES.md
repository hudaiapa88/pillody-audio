## Use rules for Mixkit sounds

- YouTube: only as one layer of a mixed soundscape under our own picture; never a single Mixkit sound as the whole video.
- Descriptions say "sound mixed by Pillody", never "recorded by Pillody".
- Never submit these sounds (or videos built on them) to Content ID or any rights-management service.
- The app is a tool that plays these sounds on their own: replace them with CC0 or self-made sounds.
- New sounds: CC0 (record URL, author, licence, date, original file name, sha256) or synthesised in code.

## DRONE-* pads (own synthesis)

DRONE-01..04 were synthesised with ffmpeg by us (pillody_ses_varliklari.md §4 "ffmpeg sentez (432 Hz)"; originals carry
only an ffmpeg encoder tag and are exactly 45 s). Measured on 2026-11-10 against the spec: DRONE-01 60 Hz + 30 Hz
sub-octave, nothing above 500 Hz; DRONE-02 432/216/864 Hz; DRONE-03 216/432/864 Hz; DRONE-04 216/432 Hz, nothing above
500 Hz. No third-party licence applies. FREQ-*, SWEEP-*, NOISE-* and BREATH-* are also generated by build_tracks.py.
